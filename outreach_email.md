## Notes that matter

- **Answer the "how big are you" question honestly.** Say the real number. Small
  and honest beats vague and inflated, and they will check.
- **Lead with what they get** (traffic, a free listing), not with what you want.
- **One ask per email.** Version A asks two things, which is already the limit.
- **Reply-to must be a person**, not info@.

## Sending, in order

1. Warm up the domain: 10-20 emails a day for the first two weeks.
2. Start with the 30 brands you actually like and already stock in the feed.
3. Wait a week. Reply to anyone who answers before adding more.
4. Only scale up once you know which subject line gets opened.
